# Welcome to My Blog

Hello! I'm excited to share my journey and insights about building software for Apple platforms.

## What You'll Find Here

In this blog, I'll be writing about:

- **iOS Development**: Best practices, SwiftUI patterns, and lessons learned from building production apps
- **Testing & Quality**: How to build robust test automation frameworks and maintain high-quality standards
- **CI/CD**: Strategies for effective continuous integration and deployment for mobile apps
- **Career Growth**: Reflections on transitioning from quality engineering to software development

## My Approach

I believe in building apps that work reliably from day one. My background in software quality engineering gives me a unique perspective on development - I think about edge cases, error handling, and user experience from the start, not as an afterthought.

## Stay Tuned

I'll be sharing practical insights, code examples, and real-world experiences from working on apps serving millions of devices at Jamf, as well as my indie app development journey with MovingBox and other projects.
